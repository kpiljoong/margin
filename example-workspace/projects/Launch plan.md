# Launch plan
We are launching the private beta in October with a small group of writers and engineers who already keep notes in Markdown.

## Goals
* Validate that people trust an agent more when every change is reviewable.
* Get 30 weekly active users.
+ Learn which recipes people reuse.
##Timeline
| Week | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| 1 | Invite first 10 users |
| 2 | Collect feedback |

## Risks
- Agents rewriting too much at once → keep scopes small by default.   
- Privacy concerns → private notes are withheld, see [[Welcome]].
